# AI Governance and Security Policy

## Purpose
This document defines the governance framework for this repository with respect to artificial intelligence (AI) and automated systems. It outlines our stance, monitoring practices, and enforcement mechanisms.

## Core Principles
1. **Human‑First Access** – This repository is designed for human developers and researchers, not for AI training or automated data harvesting.
2. **Zero Tolerance for AI Scraping** – We prohibit any AI‑based cloning, crawling, or data extraction without explicit written permission.
3. **Transparency** – We monitor public activity (e.g., forks, clones, and API requests) to detect and respond to policy violations.

## Monitoring & Logging
- We may log IP addresses, user agents, and access times via GitHub's native audit logs and external analytics.
- We actively monitor fork counts and unusual spikes in traffic that may indicate scraping.

## Enforcement Actions
Violations of this governance policy may result in:
- Issuance of a formal cease‑and‑desist notice.
- Filing of DMCA takedown requests with hosting providers.
- Legal action under applicable laws (including the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act or equivalent).
